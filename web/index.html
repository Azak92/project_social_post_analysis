What makes a voice?

What these measurements tell you

Capitalization and punctuation describe emphasis. Word length describes surface complexity. Unique-word share changes strongly with text length: compare posts of similar lengths.

These are descriptive features, not intelligence scores, English proficiency levels, or proof of who wrote a post. Semantic similarity and validated readability analysis come next.
